The idea

Make the complexity
feel like second nature.

Flower is full of nuance. A name and a THC percentage tell only part of its story. The Flower Spectrum gives aroma a visual language: ten profiles, a consistent palette, and a fingerprint that makes each expression distinct.

I designed the system to travel. From the first moment of discovery to a shelf card, an educational guide, or a cultivator’s social post, the same visual cues help people find their bearings.

What’s behind the fingerprint?+

A fixed visual grammar. Each profile owns one of ten sectors. Length shows strength relative to the leading profile; the largest sector always reaches the outer ring. Sector position never changes.

A quieter shorthand. The strip includes up to three profiles scoring at least 60% of the leader. Segment widths are proportional within that selected group, so the strip and fingerprint express different levels of detail.

An interpretive model. The supplied prototype weights terpene values into aroma profiles. These are model scores, not measured aroma percentages. Historical panels come from the supplied prototype; original lab reports were not provided.

Aroma exploration, not effects prediction or medical guidance. Sample dates: July 2023. This is a portfolio demonstration.
